The Studio

Yougita Shinde

Founder & Principal Designer

A qualified bachelor in interior design and a certified Vastu Visharad from the Institute of Oriental Science, Pune, Yougita founded Ccreative Interiors in 2015. With 18+ years of hands-on experience, her studio has become a trusted name across residential, commercial and institutional design.

  • — Awarded by Ex-Minister Shri Dilip Walse Patil for exceptional commercial interior work in Pune
  • — Appointed Interior Designer for the Commissioner’s Bungalow, Navi Mumbai
  • — Designed Ratnagiri Medical College premises — classrooms and administrative offices
  • — Designed the residence of the Former Secretary of the P.W.D., Maharashtra
